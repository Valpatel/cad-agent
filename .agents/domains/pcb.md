# Electronics evidence

KiCad is the native electrical authority. CAD pass, source freshness, physical fit, electrical qualification and supplier readiness are separate facts. Consume native JSON; do not reduce DRC to its `violations` array and lose unconnected/parity findings. Exact MPN plus manufacturer plus supplier code matters; short names such as SM712 are not substitution approval.
