**Changed**
- `cadagent.review.gates.relations.relate` and the relation table measure the distance between two solids faster (#186). On solids, `BRepExtrema_DistShapeShape` first checks whether any vertex of one lies inside the other. OCCT 7.9 builds a new point classifier for each small chunk of vertices, which was most of the time of an apart pair of many-faced parts. The gates now run that check once, with one classifier per solid. When no vertex is inside, neither part has a free edge or vertex and the faces are clear of each other, they take BRepExtrema's distance between the faces, which gives the same gap and points. Every relation, witness included, is byte-identical to before on the 811 pairs of the README hero's clearance sweep on Gyropup's leg. The sweep takes 685 s of calls instead of 951 s (183 s to 134 s on 6 workers). Pairs of many-faced links run up to 2.7× faster, and a synthetic pocketed block about 5×. Pairs whose time is BRepExtrema's own search gain little. Cached relations stay valid.
