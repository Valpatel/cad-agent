**New**
- `cadagent.mechanical.gears`: gear solids a build script can place, check and export. `spur_gear` and `external_ring_gear` make external involute gears from module (or `module_from_diametral_pitch`), tooth count, face width and pressure angle (20° by default, 14.5° works), with optional bore, backlash allowance, clearance, root fillet and profile shift; `bevel_gear` makes a straight bevel gear for 90° shafts by Tredgold's approximation. Teeth are drawn as the basic rack cuts them: true involute flanks, the trochoid root fillet, and undercut on low tooth counts drawn and reported (`undercut`, a `UserWarning`). `spur_gear_dimensions`, `gear_pair_dimensions` and `bevel_gear_dimensions` return the diameters, tooth thickness, centre distance, contact ratio and mesh rotation to place a pair by. Geometry only: no helical or internal gears, no tip relief, no strength check, and printed gears need their backlash and clearance tuned to the printer (`docs/mechanical/gears.md`, #723).
