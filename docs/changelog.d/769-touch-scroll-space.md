**Fixed**
- `<cad-model>` (`kit/model/model.js`): on a touch screen a viewer now takes vertical drags only while it is at most half as tall as the space that scrolls past it: the visible viewport, or the nearest scrollable box around it when that is shorter. What lies beside a viewer is no longer counted as free, because it may be another viewer. With two viewers side by side in phone landscape (844×390, 740×360, 932×430) a vertical swipe on the home page layout now scrolls from all 35 sampled start points, where before it scrolled from 5 (the gutters). Sideways drags still turn those viewers. A viewer nearly as tall as its scrollable box lets the box scroll again (#769).
- `<cad-model>`: a second finger on the canvas ends a one-finger turn and is itself ignored, so a pinch zooms the page and leaves the view alone (#769).
- Limits: checked with emulated touch in desktop Chromium only, not in Safari, Chrome on Android or on a physical phone. Two viewers stacked with nothing between them can still cover most of a screen (#769).
