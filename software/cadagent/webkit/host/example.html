Try the controls

Drag to orbit. Use two fingers to pan or pinch. Focus a camera and use arrow keys to orbit, Shift + arrows to pan, +/− to zoom, and Home to fit. Open the inspector for part selection and display controls.

This is a presentation example, not a mechanical qualification. Selections identify objects in the currently loaded document; they do not imply a cross-document engineering relationship.
