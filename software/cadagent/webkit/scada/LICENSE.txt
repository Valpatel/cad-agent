SCADA package terms
===================

their declared private: true and UNLICENSED terms. All rights are reserved
unless a separate applicable permission or license states otherwise.

The project maintainer authorized physical integration of this source and
its public presentation in cad-agent. That authorization is recorded in
SOURCE.json. It does not silently relicense these packages under the MIT
license used by the surrounding cad-agent toolkit.

Source identities and the original inventory digest are retained in the SCADA
root SOURCE.json; the initial import and subsequent changes remain in Git history.
Third-party dependencies and notices keep their own terms. Reference links
identify studied sources; they do not grant redistribution rights to those
external documents.
