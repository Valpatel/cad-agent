# Power-sector design references

Reviewed 2026-11-13 for the proposed [power-sector expansion](https://github.com/mvalancy/digester-insight/blob/main/docs/coverage/power-sector-plan.md). These references inform equipment names, recognizable geometry, media and structural connections. They do not validate the project's placeholder equations, capacities, efficiencies, protection logic or incident probabilities. All new illustrations should remain original artwork; do not copy agency diagrams or vendor imagery into the package.

The entries below distinguish material actually opened/read from inaccessible follow-up references. Publication dates belong to the source; the review date is not a claim that historical performance or project-status statements remain current.

## Evidence boundaries for implementation

- Store a stable reference ID, exact URL, section/page locator, review date and supported claim with each proposed equipment family or example. “Reference-derived structure” and “illustrative dynamics” are independent provenance fields.
- A rendered pipe path is an authoring choice; the reference supports its media relationship, not the exact geometry. Reference diagrams do not establish the project's invented plant tags, setpoints or failure rates.
- Do not turn an old guide's performance examples into current vendor specifications. Rated capacity presets must say “illustrative preset” until tied to a specific verified source/model.
- Nuclear examples remain conceptual heat-transfer diagrams. Detailed core design, neutronics, protection settings and licensed operating procedures are outside this implementation plan.
