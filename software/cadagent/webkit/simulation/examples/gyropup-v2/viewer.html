Source, method and limits

Gyropup's recorded MuJoCo joint frames (30 per second) are posed through the rig's own five-bar and turret kinematics, and the rig's meshes are packed for the browser. No physics runs here; playback interpolates between recorded frames and does not enforce contacts or joint constraints between them. CAD and simulation do not prove physical fit, strength or control safety.
